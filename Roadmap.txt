The 5 Neutral Natures (Balanced / Unpredictable)

Hardy
While Charging: Moves steadily forward down the lane at a moderate pace, ignoring minor positioning shifts.
Post-Charge: Takes the first valid target in range straight ahead; if none, resumes steady advance.
Docile
While Charging: Mimics the movement of the nearest ally mon, staying roughly parallel or trailing slightly behind them.
Post-Charge: Targets whatever enemy the ally it's following is currently targeting (if valid), otherwise targets closest enemy.
Bashful
While Charging: Frequently switches behavior—takes a few steps forward, then stutters back or shifts sideways to adjacent rows.
Post-Charge: Executes attack nervously; if multiple targets, hesitates briefly before picking one randomly.
Quirky
While Charging: Moves in erratic zig-zags across the 3 rows, changing columns randomly every turn.
Post-Charge: Fires immediately at whatever random target is currently in range, regardless of how optimal it is.
Serious
While Charging: Takes the absolute shortest precomputed path directly toward its preliminary target.
Post-Charge: Fires instantly upon the primary target; if out of range, moves straight to it without deviating.

The Attack-Boosted Natures (Aggressive / Forward-Pressuring)
Lonely
While Charging: Rushes forward aggressively, but tends to drift toward the edges of the 3-row grid as if "isolated."
Post-Charge: Immediately attacks the nearest enemy, prioritizing targets that are isolated from other enemies.
Brave
While Charging: Charges head-on down the middle row (Row 2), marching straight into the thick of the enemy team.
Post-Charge: Fires at the strongest-looking enemy or the one furthest ahead in the enemy lines.
Adamant
While Charging: Marches relentlessly toward the closest enemy, refusing to step back even if blocked or taking damage.
Post-Charge: Executes attack with zero hesitation on the first available target in range.
Naughty
While Charging: Wanders into blind spots or behind obstacles near the enemy lines to flank them.
Post-Charge: Targets enemies unpredictably, favoring targets with low health or annoying status effects.

The Defense-Boosted Natures (Cautious / Frontline Anchors)
Bold
While Charging: Steps forward to claim the front tiles of its side, then holds its ground stubbornly.
Post-Charge: Attacks the closest approaching enemy to push them back.
Relaxed
While Charging: Barely moves at all; if forced to move by obstacles, takes the slowest possible drift.
Post-Charge: Waits until an enemy walks directly into its move's optimal range before firing.
Impish
While Charging: Bounces back and forth between two adjacent tiles, micro-adjusting position playfully.
Post-Charge: Prefers targeting enemies that haven't attacked yet or are moving around.
Lax
While Charging: Stays completely still, effectively acting as a stationary turret while charging.
Post-Charge: Fires on whatever target wanders into range; if none, simply stays put and waits for the next cycle.

The Speed-Boosted Natures (Hit-and-Run / Evasive)
Timid
While Charging: Retreats backward toward its own board edge, maximizing distance from enemies while charging.
Post-Charge: Fires max-range moves safely from afar; if an enemy gets too close, immediately steps back.
Hasty
While Charging: Sprints diagonally back and forth across the 3 rows at maximum movement speed.
Post-Charge: Fires the instant the charge completes, rushing forward just enough to hit and then dodging away.
Jolly
While Charging: Constantly shifts forward and backward, maintaining a lively, energetic pacing.
Post-Charge: Targets enemies dynamically, bouncing to a new tile right after the attack executes.
Naive
While Charging: Runs carelessly right past enemies into the enemy backline, ignoring danger zones.
Post-Charge: Turns around wildly and fires at whatever target is closest behind or beside it.

The Special Attack-Boosted Natures (Calculated / Area-Focused)

Modest
While Charging: Moves steadily to the center row to maximize the potential coverage area of its moves.
Post-Charge: Deliberately targets groups of enemies or lines up AoE/beam attacks for maximum collateral.
Mild
While Charging: Creeps forward cautiously, halting its movement the moment an enemy enters its field of view.
Post-Charge: Executes attack carefully, backing away slightly if the target is too close for comfort.
Quiet
While Charging: Slows its movement down to a crawl, focusing entirely on internal timing.
Post-Charge: Unleashes its move with pinpoint precision on the highest-priority target in range.
Rash
While Charging: Dashes forward haphazardly toward the largest cluster of enemies on the map.
Post-Charge: Fires immediately at the closest target, completely unconcerned about hitting friendlies (if friendly fire is a factor) or collateral.

The Special Defense-Boosted Natures (Tactical / Defensive Positioning)
Calm
While Charging: Smoothly slides behind obstacles or allies to use them as physical cover.
Post-Charge: Calmly selects a target and executes the move with clinical precision.
Gentle

Post-Charge: Attacks defensive or support-oriented targets if available.

While Charging: Stays put or takes a slow, mocking step backward, facing the enemy defiantly.
